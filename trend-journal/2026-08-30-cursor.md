# SizeShift Packet — Current-vs-proposed SBA size-status mapping + affiliation evidence for US federal contractors — 2026-08-30

**Contributor:** Cursor
**File:** `trend-journal/2026-08-30-cursor.md`
**Market:** United States first; Indonesia relevance: not yet established (SBA size standards and SAM self-certification are US federal programs)
**Motion:** B2B. A US federal contractor (owner, contracts manager, or outside bookkeeper/consultant acting for them) pays. Capture/BD and finance staff use it. GovCon counsel and PTAC advisors are referral intermediaries, not the payer for v1.
**Verdict:** Watch

## Summary

On 20 August 2026 SBA published a proposed rule that would consolidate nearly 1,000 NAICS-level size standards into **338** group-level standards, shift many industries to employee-based measures, and newly classify about **114,541** firms as small—including roughly **37,002** FY 2025 federal contractors holding more than **$71 billion** in contracts. Comments close **21 September 2026**. Law firms are already advising contractors to map current NAICS codes to proposed groupings and to model affiliate-inclusive size under both regimes without changing SAM representations yet.

**SizeShift Packet** would be a Stripe-billed web app that (1) loads a contractor’s NAICS portfolio, (2) shows current vs proposed thresholds and measure flips (receipts ↔ employees), (3) maintains an affiliate/entity ledger with receipt and headcount inputs, and (4) exports a dated size-status evidence packet for SAM annual recertification, offer-time representations, and comment-letter prep. The durable job is **affiliate-inclusive size evidence**, not a one-shot NPRM calculator. Verdict is **Watch**, not Build: SBA already ships a free Size Standards Tool and affiliation compliance guide; the NPRM adds no new recordkeeping mandate; high-stakes size opinions remain counsel work; and no prepaid interviews yet show subscription WTP versus Excel + PTAC + a law-firm memo.

## Inference

- The NPRM is a **durable regulatory rewrite process**, not a one-day news spike: comments close in three weeks, a final rule (if issued) will force every SAM-registered contractor to re-evaluate size under new thresholds/measures, and annual SAM self-certification plus offer-time size representations already create a recurring workflow.
- Attention and willingness to pay for a **temporary “proposed vs current” mapper** will peak through the comment period and again around any final-rule effective date, then collapse unless the product owns the always-on **affiliation ledger + evidence packet** job.
- Free SBA tools close the “what is my threshold?” question. The residual gap is operational: multi-entity affiliate graphs, five-year receipts / 24-month employee averages, NAICS portfolio under both current and proposed regimes, dated exports for counsel/PTAC, and change logs when ownership or management shifts. That gap is real but may be closed by spreadsheets + counsel retainers.
- Product risk is high if the app appears to **opine** on size status or affiliation; safe positioning is evidence assembly and scenario math with explicit “not legal advice / not an SBA determination” framing. Unauthorized-practice-of-law risk is a kill switch if buyers expect a determination letter.
- Indonesia is not a secondary launch market: buyers are US SAM entities. Indonesian exporters selling into the US face customs/duty jobs, not SBA size standards.

## Assumptions

- A meaningful set of mid-size professional-services / IT / construction contractors will pay $49–$149/month (or a $299 one-time impact packet) to maintain affiliate-inclusive size evidence rather than only using Excel and episodic counsel.
- Proposed-rule tables can be licensed/ reproduced from public FR/SBA materials without becoming stale before a final rule diverges.
- Buyers will enter affiliate financials manually or via CSV; bank/tax API ingestion is out of scope for MVP and may be refused for sensitivity reasons.
- PTAC offices, fractional CFOs, and boutique GovCon consultants will refer tools if the packet reduces their assembly time without competing with legal opinions.
- Final-rule timing is uncertain; a multi-year delay or a substantially revised final table would shrink the near-term wedge to the evergreen affiliation desk alone.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

Size standards are a standing statutory apparatus (Small Business Jobs Act rolling reviews). The August 2026 NPRM is a structural methodology rewrite (338 standards, measure flips, large threshold increases), so demand to **re-map eligibility** should recur at finalization—not only during the 30-day comment window. Search-trend durability is **unverified** here (Trends explore 429). Durability of a SaaS business still depends on winning the evergreen affiliation-evidence job after the news cycle ends.

### Customer and JTBD

**Motion:** B2B.  
**Buyer:** owner or contracts lead at a US firm with active or planned small-business set-aside work (especially firms near current ceilings, recently “other than small,” or with complex ownership).  
**User:** that buyer and/or their bookkeeper.  
**JTBD:** before SAM renewal, an offer, a recertification event, or a comment deadline, produce an affiliate-inclusive, NAICS-specific size calculation with a dated evidence packet—and, through late 2026, a side-by-side view of status under **current** vs **proposed** standards without incorrectly updating SAM.

### Pain evidence

Law-firm alerts treat NAICS mapping and affiliate-inclusive modeling as immediate homework for thousands of contractors. SBA’s own affiliation guide exists because control-based affiliation is easy to miss. Pain is **process and error-cost** (lost set-asides, protest vulnerability, False Claims Act exposure for knowing misrepresentation)—not a missing free threshold lookup. Direct buyer complaints and prepaid WTP are **not** yet verified in this entry.

### Alternatives

- SBA Size Standards Tool + Table of Size Standards (free).
- SBA affiliation compliance guide + DIY spreadsheet.
- GovCon counsel / size-protest defense firms (high-touch, high-ticket).
- Full GovCon suites / capture platforms (GovWin-class) that may absorb NAICS notes without specializing in affiliation ledgers.
- PTAC counseling (free/low-cost guidance, not a multi-tenant SaaS packet).

**Gap to test:** a cheap, always-on **affiliate ledger + dual-regime mapper + exportable evidence packet** that counsel can review—not another NAICS lookup clone.

### Wedge

v1: US professional-services and IT contractors with **2–15 related entities** (common ownership, shared management, or JV/mentor-protégé ties) who need a current-vs-proposed map for the Sept 2026 comment window and a reusable packet for 2026–2027 SAM/offer cycles. Explicitly **not** an automated size determination, not 8(a) application software, and not a full capture CRM.

### MVP

- Import NAICS list + primary code; show current threshold/measure from SBA table.
- Overlay proposed 4-/5-digit group standard and flag measure flips (receipts ↔ employees) from published NPRM tables.
- Affiliate/entity roster with ownership/control notes, receipts by fiscal year, and average employee counts.
- Compute affiliate-inclusive totals vs each standard; generate a dated PDF/CSV packet (inputs, math, sources, disclaimer).
- Optional comment-letter appendix export for regulations.gov docket SBA-2026-0199.
- Stripe Checkout: $79 one-time “Impact Packet” or $99/month workspace.

### AI role

AI drafts plain-language summaries of which NAICS move groups, flags missing affiliate years, and turns structured inputs into a readable packet narrative. It must **not** invent affiliation conclusions, control findings, or SBA determinations. Durable value is the ledger, dual tables, and audit trail—not a “size GPT.”

### Pricing

**Hypothesis, unverified:** $79 prepaid impact packet (one portfolio, one export) as the falsifier; then $99/month for living affiliate ledger + re-exports. Anchors against a $1.5k–$5k counsel memo for mapping-only work, not against free SBA tools.

### Distribution

- SEO/content around “SBA size standards 2026 proposed rule” and NAICS-specific pages (5415, 5413, construction employee conversions) during the comment window.
- Outreach to PTACs and boutique GovCon CFOs with a free static mapper and paid packet.
- LinkedIn / association lists for mid-tier IT and engineering contractors that recently graduated from small status.

### Risks

- **Market:** Free official tools + Excel close the thin wedge; WTP collapses after comments close.
- **Product:** Final rule diverges from proposal; table maintenance burden.
- **Legal:** Product mistaken for legal advice; FCA sensitivity around size representations.
- **GTM:** Law firms and PTACs may refuse to refer a tool that looks like it replaces opinions.
- **Technical:** Scraping/keeping proposed vs current tables accurate; multi-entity math edge cases (JVs, ostensible subcontracting) out of MVP scope.

### Kill criteria

Stop if any of the following hold within two weeks of outreach (before building beyond a static mapper):

1. Fewer than **10** prepaid $79 impact packets from target contractors after 40 qualified conversations.
2. ≥70% of interested buyers say Excel + free SBA tool is “good enough” once shown the packet mock.
3. A credible SMB-priced incumbent already sells the same affiliate-ledger + dual-regime packet.
4. Counsel review concludes the MVP creates unacceptable UPL / misrepresentation risk under the planned disclaimer.
