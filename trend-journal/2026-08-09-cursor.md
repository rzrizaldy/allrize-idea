# IrisPrep — 2026-08-09

**Contributor:** Cursor (Grok)
**File:** `trend-journal/2026-08-09-cursor.md`
**Market:** United States first; Indonesia relevance: not yet established (IRS information-return rails do not transfer)
**Motion:** B2B — US small businesses, bookkeepers, and boutique CPA firms pay; AP/ops staff and owners use the product to collect W-9s, validate TINs/names, obtain an IRIS TCC, and prepare CSV/XML for filing
**Verdict:** Reject

## Summary

IrisPrep would be a Stripe-billed year-round contractor W-9 / TIN vault plus IRIS Transmitter Control Code (TCC) onboarding checklist and IRS Taxpayer Portal CSV packager for US SMBs and bookkeepers facing the FIRE → IRIS cutover. The regulatory deadline is real and near: FIRE stops accepting submissions on **19 November 2026**, and IRIS is the only electronic information-return intake path after **1 January 2027** for tax year 2026 filings. That is durable process change, not a fad. It is still **not** a solo-SaaS Build. The IRS already ships a **free** IRIS Taxpayer Portal (manual entry + CSV upload, payee copies, issuer records) once a filer has an IRIS TCC; Tax1099 markets pay-as-you-go filing from about **$2.99/form** with a free Essential tier and claims an IRIS migration already complete; Avalara 1099 & W-9 (Track1099) sells the same year-round W-9 + TIN-match + e-file loop. A CSV helper or TCC checklist alone is a thin wedge against that free portal plus priced incumbents. No idea-grill promotion.

## Verified evidence

### IRS FIRE retirement and IRIS mandate

- Collected **2026-08-09** from the IRS FIRE page ([Filing Information Returns Electronically (FIRE)](https://www.irs.gov/e-file-providers/filing-information-returns-electronically-fire), page last reviewed/updated **07-Aug-2026**):
  - New IR Applications for FIRE TCCs are no longer accepted; existing FIRE apps become read-only after December 2026.
  - Current FIRE users must complete an **IRIS Application for TCC**; FIRE TCCs are not interchangeable with IRIS.
  - Key dates: **1 Nov 2026** last FIRE test filings; **9 Nov 2026** last FIRE IR Application changes; **19 Nov 2026, 3 p.m. ET** last day to file through FIRE.
  - After **1 Jan 2027**, IRIS is the only electronic information-return system for current year, prior year, and corrections.
- Collected **2026-08-09** from [E-file information returns with IRIS](https://www.irs.gov/filing/e-file-information-returns-with-iris) (page last reviewed/updated **04-Aug-2026**):
  - Two channels: **IRIS Taxpayer Portal/UI** (free) and **IRIS A2A**.
  - Taxpayer Portal: e-file up to **100 returns at a time**, manual entry or **.csv upload**, download payee copies, keep filed/distributed records, manage issuer info; requires an IRIS TCC.
  - A2A: for third-party software/services; up to **100 MB** per transmission after TCC, API Client ID, schema package, and ATS testing ([Publication 5718](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5718.pdf) referenced on the page).
  - Electronic filing is required when filing **10 or more** information returns (tax year 2023+ rule restated on the page).
- Collected **2026-08-09** from IRS [Publication 5717](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5717.pdf) (Rev. **2-2026**, downloaded same day): the Taxpayer Portal is described as a **no-cost** online method; CSV upload supports IRS-provided templates with a maximum of **250 records per file** (header excluded). Sovos summarized the 100→250 CSV increase for Processing Year 2026 on **2026-01-27**: [IRS Revises IRIS Taxpayer Portal User Guide](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/trr/irs-revises-iris-taxpayer-portal-user-guide-for-processing-year-2026/).
- Collected **2026-08-09** from [IRIS application for TCC](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/iris-application-for-tcc) (page last reviewed/updated **02-Apr-2026**): applicants choose issuer, transmitter, and/or software-developer roles; each authorized user needs an account; TCC is IRIS-only.

## Inference

- The durable shift is **validation-first information-return filing** under IRIS, plus the hard FIRE shutdown calendar — not a temporary AI or search spike (Trends unavailable here).
- For filers under ~250 forms, the IRS Taxpayer Portal already closes “upload CSV and file” once a TCC exists. A solo product that only formats CSVs or hosts a TCC checklist competes with **$0 official software**.
- The economically interesting job is **year-round W-9 collection + TIN/name match + multi-state furnish + corrections**, which Avalara and Tax1099 already price for SMBs (pay-per-form from low single-digit dollars; free Essential on Tax1099). Becoming another Authorized E-file transmitter with IRIS A2A + ATS is a regulated integration project, not a weekend Stripe MVP advantage.
- Legacy open-source FIRE generators will **mislead** builders who ship Pub 1220 flat files into a 2027 season that no longer accepts FIRE — a technical trap, not a moat.
- Indonesia is not a credible secondary market for v1: the buyer, rails, and penalties are US IRS-specific.

## Assumptions

- A meaningful set of US SMBs will refuse Tax1099/Avalara and prefer DIY portal filing even after CSV rejection friction (unverified; Tax1099 marketing asserts the opposite).
- Bookkeepers would pay a separate monthly SaaS for W-9 vaulting when QuickBooks, Bill.com, Avalara, and Tax1099 already collect W-9s (unverified).
- Solo founders can obtain and maintain IRIS transmitter / software-developer TCCs and ATS approval fast enough to matter before Nov 2026 (timeline risk; IRS materials note multi-week TCC processing).
- State information-return formats remaining on FIRE-like paths create a dual-rail pain that a new entrant can uniquely solve (plausible as Avalara/Tax1099 marketing, not independently measured here).
- Willingness to pay **$29–$79/month** for an IrisPrep vault without e-file authority has not been validated with deposits or signed pilots.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

FIRE → IRIS is a multi-decade infrastructure replacement with dated shutdown milestones on IRS.gov. Durability is high for the **workflow**, not for a greenfield product category. Search interest is unknown (Trends 429).

### Customer and JTBD

- **Motion:** B2B.
- **Who pays:** owner or bookkeeper/CPA serving US SMBs that issue ≥10 Forms 1099-NEC/MISC (and related) annually.
- **Who uses:** AP clerk, office manager, or bookkeeper assembling payee data and filings.
- **JTBD:** before filing season, have complete W-9s, TIN/name pairs that survive IRIS validation, an IRIS TCC (or a provider that holds one), and a clean submission path with recipient copies and correction handling.

### Pain evidence

IRS primary docs prove forced migration and new credentials. Avalara’s August 2026 article documents the shift from post-file correction to at-submission validation and lists common W-9 failure modes. That supports **process urgency**. It does **not** prove willingness to pay a *new* subscription when free portal + ~$3/form incumbents exist.

### Alternatives

| Alternative | Gap vs IrisPrep hypothesis |
| --- | --- |
| Free IRIS Taxpayer Portal + IRS CSV templates | Already files; needs TCC; batch limits (100 at a time / 250 per CSV) |
| Tax1099 Essential / Teams / Scale | Pay-per-form from ~$2.99; claims IRIS-ready; W-9 + TIN tools |
| Avalara 1099 & W-9 (Track1099) | Year-round W-9, TIN match, e-file; no customer TCC required |
| QuickBooks / ERP 1099 exports + provider | Occupies data source; often hands off e-file |
| Spreadsheet + January scramble | Painful but free until rejection costs appear |
| OSS FIRE generators (moov-io/irs, fire-1099) | Wrong rail after FIRE retirement |

### Wedge

Narrow wedge tested and **rejected**: “IRIS CSV packager + TCC checklist cheaper than Tax1099.” The free portal already packs CSV; Tax1099’s free Essential + per-form pricing undercuts a $29+/mo subscription for seasonal filers; Avalara owns the year-round W-9 story IrisPrep would need to differentiate.

### MVP

Not recommended. If forced: multi-tenant W-9 request links, TIN format checks (not IRS TIN Match without authorization), IRIS CSV export, TCC application checklist, Stripe $39/mo — still loses to free portal + Tax1099 on filing day.

### AI role

At most: map messy vendor CSVs to IRS template columns and flag sole-proprietor name/TIN shape issues. That is assistive cleanup, not a defendable product without filing authority or TIN Match. Generic “AI tax chatbot” would fail the scout quality bar.

### Pricing

Hypothesized $29–$79/mo or $99/season is **not credible** against $0 portal filing and ~$3/form managed e-file unless interviews show prepaid demand for a vault that those tools do not cover. No deposits collected in this scout.

### Distribution

Bookkeeper Facebook groups, r/Bookkeeping, Tipalti/Bill.com partner directories, and “FIRE retirement” SEO — the same channels Tax1099/Avalara already dominate with IRIS content. No unique owned channel identified.

### Risks

- **Market:** free official portal + entrenched per-form incumbents.
- **Product:** shipping FIRE-format OSS into an IRIS-only season.
- **Technical/legal:** IRIS A2A ATS, transmitter obligations, TIN Match authorization, PII retention.
- **GTM:** seasonal usage → high churn for pure subscription packaging.
- **Timing:** TCC lead time vs Nov 2026 FIRE hard stop creates scramble demand that incumbents absorb.

### Kill criteria

Any one of the following should stop the idea (already met for Build):

1. Free IRIS Taxpayer Portal continues to support CSV upload + payee copies for the target volume band.
2. Tax1099 or Avalara remains IRIS-ready at published SMB prices ≤ ~$3/form without requiring customers to hold their own TCC.
3. Five structured interviews fail to produce prepaid deposits for a vault that does **not** include Authorized E-file submission.
4. Builder cannot articulate a wedge that is neither “cheaper Tax1099” nor “AI wrapper on IRS PDFs.”

## Verdict rationale

**Reject.** The IRIS transition is a real, dated, US-first compliance event with clear JTBD language — but the official free portal plus Tax1099 and Avalara already monetize (or zero-price) the filing job. Promoting to idea-grill would violate the scout rule against building when a free government portal and named SMB-priced incumbents occupy the exact wedge. Revisit only if IRS removes free CSV filing **and** interviews show prepaid demand for a transmitter-grade niche (e.g. multi-EIN bookkeeper desk) that incumbents demonstrably refuse to serve.
