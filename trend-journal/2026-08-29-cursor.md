# HeatCare Packet — Vacant / unoccupied heat-maintenance evidence for small US landlords and STR hosts — 2026-08-29

**Contributor:** Cursor (Grok scheduled daily SaaS scout)
**File:** `trend-journal/2026-08-29-cursor.md`
**Market:** United States first. Indonesia relevance: **not established** — freeze/pipe-burst “maintain heat or drain” policy exclusions are a cold-climate insurance construct; Indonesia has no credible secondary-market angle for this wedge.
**Motion:** B2B / B2C hybrid. The **buyer who pays** is a small landlord, second-home owner, or STR co-host/operator (1–20 doors). The **user** is the same person or a caretaker who logs visits and thermostat proof. Insurers and adjusters consume the packet but do not pay. Not B2B2C: no intermediary partner is required to deliver value.
**Verdict:** **Watch**

## Summary

**HeatCare Packet** is a seasonal, Stripe-monetizable web workspace that helps small US property owners prove they used “reasonable care to maintain heat” (or shut off and drain water) while a dwelling was vacant or temporarily unoccupied. Before and during winter, the owner records vacancy windows, thermostat setpoints (commonly ≥55°F in industry guidance), caretaker check-ins with photos, HVAC/service receipts, and optional smart-thermostat or utility exports. On a freeze loss, the product exports a chronological claim-defense PDF aligned to the common homeowners-policy frozen-plumbing exclusion—not another leak-shutoff gadget and not a full property-ops suite.

Today’s Google Trends attention is Almanac winter-forecast curiosity, not paid demand for insurance evidence software. The underlying insurance job is durable and recurring every cold season, and case law shows documentation fights are real. The gap is willingness to pay for a narrow packet when Nest/Ecobee apps, Moen Flo / Phyn hardware, Breezeway / Turno checklists, and free winterization SOPs already cover adjacent work. Watch, not Build, until prepaid owner interviews beat free Drive folders plus existing monitoring apps.

[Almanac winter forecast](https://www.almanac.com/winter-extended-forecast-farmers-almanac)

[USA TODAY](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/weather/2026/08/07/farmers-almanac-winter-forecast-el-nino-us-weather/91210690007/)
- Together these explain today’s **20K+ / +900%** “old farmer almanac winter forecast” Trends row. They do **not** prove buyers are searching for SaaS.

### 2. Policy language creates a recurring documentation job

- Standard frozen-plumbing exclusions require the insured, while the dwelling is vacant/unoccupied, to use **reasonable care to maintain heat** **or** shut off the water supply and drain systems. Practitioner commentary on State Farm–style wording and CP 00 10 vacancy interplay is longstanding. [Property Casualty 360 / FCS Q&A](https://www.propertycasualty360.com/fcs/2021/02/16/popular-frozen-pipe-insurance-coverage-questions/); [Merlin Law Group — duty to maintain heat](https://www.propertyinsurancecoveragelaw.com/blog/frozen-pipe-exclusion-and-the-duty-to-maintain-heat/)
- Industry guidance commonly treats **~55°F** as a practical heat floor for “reasonable care,” though that number is often **not** written into the policy form itself. [Hilb Group](https://www.hilbgroup.com/blog/does-home-insurance-cover-frozen-pipes/); [Property Casualty 360](https://www.propertycasualty360.com/fcs/2021/02/16/popular-frozen-pipe-insurance-coverage-questions/)
- Broker guidance flags vacant / extended-absence properties as higher denial risk when heat is off or winterization is undocumented. [Hilb Group](https://www.hilbgroup.com/blog/does-home-insurance-cover-frozen-pipes/)

### 3. Courts still litigate “reasonable care” on facts and records

- In *Zimmerman v. Leatherstocking Cooperative Insurance Co.* (N.Y. App. Div., April 18, 2024), the insured left a Saratoga Springs home unoccupied; a radiator pipe froze; the carrier denied for failure to maintain heat or drain. The appellate court kept summary judgment off the table despite evidence of insulation, an operational heating system, and **thermostats set to 55°F**, citing disputed facts on boiler function and monitoring arrangements. Cozen O’Connor summary collected 2026-08-29: [Property Insurance Law Observer](https://www.propertyinsurancelawobserver.com/2024/05/21/what-is-reasonable-care-for-maintaining-heat-in-an-unoccupied-home/)
- A March 11, 2026 Washington federal order in a freeze/unoccupied dispute again centers on whether plaintiffs used reasonable care (routine checks, cabinets open for pipe heat, prior freeze-free history) under policy text that excludes freezing loss while unoccupied unless heat was maintained or water drained. [Order PDF hosted by Merlin Law Group](https://www.propertyinsurancecoveragelaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Order-RE-reasonable-care-to-maintain-heat-and-Ensuiing-Loss-Washington-Fed-Ct.pdf)
- **Inference (labeled):** documentation quality affects outcomes, but even “good” records do not guarantee coverage. A product that over-promises claim wins would be deceptive.

## Inference

- The Almanac Trends spike is a **seasonal planning cue**, not a purchase funnel. Durable demand, if any, comes from **policy exclusions + winter recurrence**, not from Almanac accuracy.
- The sharpest wedge is **claim-defense chronology for vacant/unoccupied periods**, not leak detection and not guest turnover ops. Moen Flo prevents loss; Breezeway runs crews; neither sells a $49–$99 seasonal PDF that maps visits, setpoints, and drain decisions to the exclusion text.
- Buyers with **1–5 doors** are the only plausible Stripe-first segment: too small for Aspire/Breezeway economics, large enough that one denied freeze claim hurts. Portfolio operators will already be inside a PM suite.
- AI’s only honest role is structuring logs and drafting a neutral timeline from uploaded artifacts. An “AI will get your claim paid” wrapper fails the scout quality bar and invites UDAAP / unauthorized-practice risk.
- Indonesia is irrelevant for v1; freeze exclusions do not travel to tropical markets as a secondary wedge.

## Assumptions

- A non-trivial share of small landlords / STR hosts leave units empty for days–weeks in freeze climates without draining plumbing.
- Those owners will pay **$49–$99 per property per winter season** (or ~$9–$15/mo Oct–Mar) for an evidence packet even when Nest already shows temperature history.
- Insurers and public adjusters will accept a structured PDF (timestamps, photos, utility/thermostat exports) as useful even though “reasonable care” remains fact-specific.
- Optional read-only Ecobee/Nest/Resideo OAuth is achievable without becoming a regulated insurance adviser.
- CAC via landlord Facebook groups, Reddit (r/Landlord, r/AirBnBHosts), and insurance-agent referrals can stay below ~$25 for a $79 seasonal SKU.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

**Mixed.** Consumer Almanac search interest is an annual spike with weak purchase intent. The **insurance exclusion + cold-season recurrence** is durable across decades of case law and forms. Product demand is seasonal (sell Aug–Nov; value realized Dec–Mar), which is acceptable for a solo SaaS if churn is planned as seasonal repurchase rather than monthly habit.

### Customer and JTBD

- **Motion:** B2B-leaning SMB / owner-operator (also retail B2C for second homes).
- **Buyer:** small landlord, second-home owner, or STR host who pays out of pocket (not a corporate risk budget).
- **User:** owner or hired caretaker logging visits.
- **JTBD:** “While this unit is empty this winter, help me maintain and **prove** heat (or drain), and if a pipe freezes, hand my adjuster a chronological packet that speaks the policy’s reasonable-care language.”

### Pain evidence

Verified via policy text, denial litigation (*Zimmerman*), broker articles, and restoration/PA blogs that tell owners to keep thermostat logs, utility bills, and visit records. **Unverified:** that owners will *prepay* for software before a loss rather than scramble afterward with a Google Drive folder.

### Alternatives

| Alternative | What it does | Gap vs HeatCare Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nest / Ecobee / Resideo apps | Live temp + history | Not vacancy-aware claim chronology; not drain/shutoff decision log |
| Moen Flo / Phyn | Detect/shut off water | Hardware CAPEX; does not assemble exclusion-aligned packet |
| Breezeway / Turno | Winterization tasks + photos | Ops suite priced per unit/month; not insurance-packet-first |
| Free PDF SOPs / STR Specialist checklists | Advice | No timestamps, no vault, no cold-snap prompts |
| Heat Seek / NYC HPD sensors | Tenant heat-code evidence | Inverse JTBD; dissolved nonprofit / city program |
| Spreadsheet + phone photos | Manual | Fragile under adjuster scrutiny |

### Wedge

**v1:** Freeze-climate ZIP codes only; **1–10 properties**; seasonal “HeatCare season” subscription; vacancy calendar + setpoint policy (≥55°F default, owner-editable) + caretaker visit photo log + receipt vault + one-click **Reasonable Care Packet PDF**. Explicitly **not** shutoff hardware, not guest messaging, not snow dispatch.

### MVP

Stripe Checkout seasonal plan; property profiles; vacancy date ranges; checklist templates (maintain-heat path vs drain path); mobile-friendly photo upload; optional CSV import of thermostat/utility exports; NOAA/NWS cold-alert email hooks (link out, don’t scrape illegally); PDF export with hash/timestamp footer. One strong developer can ship in a few weeks on a boring web stack.

### AI role

Classify uploads (thermostat screenshot vs utility bill vs plumber invoice); draft a neutral timeline narrative; flag gaps (“no visit logged in 14 days while vacant and outdoor forecast &lt;20°F”). Must **not** opine on coverage, invent readings, or auto-contact insurers.

### Pricing

**Hypothesis:** $79/property/winter (Oct 1–Apr 30) including unlimited PDF regenerations; $129 for 3 properties; $15 add-on caretaker seat. Concierge falsifier: sell **10 prepaid packets at $79** to landlords in OH/PA/NY/MI before writing more product.

### Distribution

1. Landlord and STR host Facebook/Reddit threads timed to Almanac/NOAA cold outlooks.  
2. Independent insurance agents / farm mutuals who already counsel 55°F setpoints—referral affiliate, not carrier partnership.  
3. HVAC contractors’ pre-season tune-up email lists (co-branded checklist landing page).  
Avoid enterprise PM RFPs.

### Risks

- **Market:** Owners treat Nest history as “good enough” until after a denial.  
- **Product:** Overclaiming coverage outcomes → legal and trust risk.  
- **Technical:** Thermostat OAuth fragility (Resideo/Google ToS); photo EXIF spoofing reduces evidence weight.  
- **GTM:** Extreme seasonality; summer near-zero engagement.  
- **Competition:** Breezeway or Steadily-class insurers ship a free “freeze care” template overnight.

### Kill criteria

Stop if any of the following holds after a bounded test:

1. **&lt;5 prepaid $79 sales** after 40 structured outreach conversations in freeze ZIPs (2-week sprint).  
2. **≥3 of 5** interviewed owners say Nest/Ecobee history + phone photos already satisfied their last adjuster ask.  
3. A named incumbent ships a **sub-$10/mo** freeze-care evidence module aimed at &lt;10-door owners.  
4. Counsel concludes the product is insurance advice / claims advocacy requiring licenses the solo founder will not obtain.
