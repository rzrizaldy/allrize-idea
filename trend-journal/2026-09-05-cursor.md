# VLI File Desk — IRS Form 1098-VLI / §6050AA SPVL interest reporting for small US auto lenders — 2026-09-05

**Contributor:** Cursor
**File:** `trend-journal/2026-09-05-cursor.md`
**Market:** United States first; Indonesia relevance: not yet established
**Motion:** B2B (interest recipient / lender pays; borrower is the recipient of the payee statement, not the buyer of the SaaS)
**Verdict:** Reject

## Summary

Treasury/IRS final regulations on the OBBBA/OBBBA-era **qualified passenger vehicle loan interest (QPVLI)** deduction and new **section 6050AA** information reporting were posted for Federal Register publication on **2026-09-08** (document **2026-18219**), effective **2026-11-09**. They finalize Form **1098-VLI** (Vehicle Loan Interest Statement) for interest recipients that, in a trade or business, receive **$600+** of interest on a specified passenger vehicle loan (SPVL) from an individual—covering new-vehicle APVs with US final assembly and original-use constraints, with the underlying deduction limited to taxable years beginning after **2024-12-31** and before **2029-01-01**. A Stripe-monetizable “upload CSV → validate VIN/assembly/eligibility → e-file 1098-VLI via IRIS → mail borrower copies” web app is technically feasible for one strong developer, but it is **not** a Build: the free **IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal** already accepts keyed or CSV information returns; dedicated and general SMB e-file vendors (**Vehicle Loan Interest**, Tax1099, 1099Pro/Boomtax-class platforms) already sell the exact filing job at commodity per-form prices (e.g. **$3/form**, **$750** annual minimum on vehicleloaninterest.com); dealer/lender DMS stacks will absorb the workflow for most of the ~**36,000** expected filers (~**24,600** small entities per the FRFA); and the statute’s pre-**2029** sunset makes a greenfield filing SaaS a short runway. Verdict: **Reject** a new 1098-VLI e-file clone. Do not promote to `idea-grill/`.

- **Statutory window and Form 1098-VLI, from same PDF (collected 2026-09-05):** OBBBA section 70203 amendments; QPVLI deduction applies solely to taxable years beginning after **2024-12-31** and before **2029-01-01**; section **6050AA(f)** provides no information return is required for periods to which section 163(h)(4) does not apply. Preamble discusses **Form 1098–VLI, Vehicle Loan Interest Statement**, including VIN, whether original use began with the purchaser, and whether final assembly occurred in the United States. Notice **2025–57** transitional relief: interest recipients deemed to satisfy 6050AA for interest received in calendar year **2025** by making a statement of total SPVL interest available to the individual (full Form 1098-VLI IRS filing was not required for that transition year under the Notice as described in the preamble).
- **§1.6050AA–1 / dealer example, from same PDF (collected 2026-09-05):** Interest recipient must file an information return and furnish a payee statement when receiving ≥$600 interest on an SPVL; regulations include a “buy here, pay here” dealer direct-lending example as an interest recipient when interest is received in the course of the auto-sales trade or business. Furnish deadline: statement to payor of record on or before **January 31** of the following year. File with IRS on or before **February 28** (**March 31** if filed electronically) of the following year (regulation text).
- **FRFA market size, from same PDF (collected 2026-09-05):** Treasury/IRS expect approximately **36,000** businesses to issue 6050AA information returns (NAICS new car dealers, motorcycle dealers, car loan lenders, consumer lending); that total **does not include used car dealers** because the statute/final regulations apply to loans for **new** vehicles; of the 36,000, about **24,600** would likely be small entities. Estimated **8,000,000** responses × **0.25** hours ≈ **2,000,000** annual burden hours. PRA points burden for 6050AA collections to Form **1098–VLI** / OMB **1545–2334**.

## Inference

- Demand for **correct SPVL interest statements** is real and statute-backed through tax years before 2029, but the **monetizable job** that a Stripe web app would sell—“generate and e-file 1098-VLI”—is already occupied by (1) free IRIS CSV and (2) multiple paid e-file vendors at low per-form prices.
- The operational hard part (VIN / final-assembly / original-use / customarily-financed allocation / which entity is the interest recipient when loans are sold or serviced) is real, yet large lenders and franchise DMS/LOS vendors have stronger distribution to own that data at origin; a standalone “eligibility AI” without DMS integration is a generic wrapper over window-sticker/VIN rules and counsel memos.
- Temporary statute (reporting ends when QPVLI ends—before **2029**) plus crowded e-file commodity economics fail the durability and wedge tests for a new solo SaaS. A Watch-only thin product (e.g. prepaid “SPVL eligibility memo + field map for one book of loans”) would still fight free IRIS + $3 e-file + CPA/DMS updates and is not strong enough for Build or idea-grill.
- Indonesia is not a secondary market for US Form 1098-VLI filing; only speculative if an Indo-owned US dealer/lender entity files US information returns (out of scope for v1 and not evidenced).

## Assumptions

- Franchise dealer DMS and major auto-finance LOS vendors will ship native 1098-VLI export/filing for the 2026 calendar-year cycle (not independently verified vendor-by-vendor in this run).
- Most of the ~24,600 small filers will satisfy the obligation via IRIS CSV, their CPA, or an existing 1099/1098 e-file vendor rather than adopt a greenfield vertical SaaS.
- Borrowers’ TurboTax/Credit Karma/CPA workflows will absorb the **deduction claiming** side; B2C “deduction maximizer” apps are a different, also crowded motion and were not the scouted wedge.
- No unexpected extension of section 163(h)(4) / 6050AA past 2028 tax years is assumed; if Congress extends the deduction, re-open only with evidence that IRIS + incumbents still leave a prepaid qualification gap.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

Statute-driven through ~2028 tax years, then scheduled to stop. Final regs (2026-18219) remove “proposed-only” ambiguity for 2026 filing season prep, but durability is **finite**, not a multi-decade compliance category like mortgage Form 1098. Trends attention on 2026-09-05 was unrelated entertainment/sports; no search-spike evidence for the form name in Trending Now.

### Customer and JTBD

**Motion:** B2B. **Buyer/payer:** controller, F&I ops, or office manager at a small US new-vehicle lender, captive-adjacent finance company, credit union auto desk, or dealer with in-house financing that receives ≥$600 SPVL interest. **User:** same ops person assembling year-end information returns. **JTBD:** before the January 31 borrower-statement and February 28 / March 31 (e-file) IRS deadlines, identify SPVLs, compute reportable interest, capture VIN/year/make/model/original-use/US-assembly fields, furnish borrower statements, and e-file with IRS without 6721/6722 penalties.

### Pain evidence

FRFA burden (~2M hours) and commenter concerns about VIN/final-assembly verification and system changes (preamble) show real compliance work. That is **not** proof of willingness to pay a new SaaS subscription once IRIS CSV and $3/form vendors exist. No primary interviews in this run.

### Alternatives

- Free **IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal** (key or CSV) and IRIS A2A for software vendors.
- **Vehicle Loan Interest** vertical e-file ($3 / $4.50 per form, $750 minimum).
- Horizontal information-return platforms (**Tax1099**, **1099Pro**, Boomtax-class).
- Dealer DMS / lender LOS / core banking year-end modules (expected).
- CPA/bookkeeper Excel + IRIS upload.
- Do nothing / miss filing (penalty exposure under 6721/6722 as amended to include 6050AA returns/statements—cited in preamble).

**Gap that remains after alternatives:** little durable white space for a new e-file UI. Any residual gap is **eligibility judgment + loan-book data hygiene**, which incumbents and DMS already market toward.

### Wedge

A hypothetical v1—“BHPH/small lender 1098-VLI desk with VIN assembly checker”—fails because (a) FRFA excludes used-car-only books from the 36k estimate for **new**-vehicle SPVLs, (b) filing is commoditized, (c) runway ends before 2029. No credible narrow wedge survives those three facts.

### MVP

(Described only to show feasibility, not recommendation.) Multi-tenant web app: Excel/CSV loan book ingest, eligibility checklist (APV, original use, US final assembly, $600 gate), Form 1098-VLI PDF/payee statement generation, IRIS CSV export or A2A transmit, Stripe Checkout for per-form or seasonal package. One strong developer can ship; **should not**.

### AI role

At most: draft eligibility explanations from structured VIN/decode + sticker fields; flag missing boxes. Must not invent final-assembly or interest amounts. Durable value would be schema + filing + audit trail—not a “car loan deduction GPT.” Even that role is insufficient given free IRIS + paid incumbents.

### Pricing

Incumbent proof: ~**$3**/form with **~$750** annual minimum already clears SMB willingness-to-pay for filing. A new entrant racing below that against a free IRS portal has no healthy SaaS margin story. Prepaid “eligibility audit” packages ($79–$299) are unvalidated and easy to substitute with CPA hours.

### Distribution

SEO on “Form 1098-VLI e-file,” partnerships with BHPH/dealer associations, CPA referral—same channels Tax1099 and vehicleloaninterest.com already work. No proprietary distribution edge identified.

### Risks

- **Market:** Temporary statute; DMS bundling; free IRIS.
- **Product:** Misclassification of SPVL/APV → customer penalty liability optics.
- **Technical:** IRIS A2A certification/ATS overhead for a short-lived form.
- **Legal:** Transmitter/issuer TCC obligations; incorrect tax advice if AI overclaims deduction eligibility.
- **GTM:** Competing with $3/form and free portal on paid ads is structurally unattractive.

### Kill criteria

Already met for Build: (1) free official CSV filing path exists; (2) ≥1 SMB-priced vendor sells the exact 1098-VLI e-file job; (3) statutory reporting sunsets with QPVLI before 2029; (4) no Trends or interview evidence of unpaid gap beyond filing. Revisit only if Congress extends 163(h)(4) **and** IRIS drops portal support **and** prepaid interviews show ≥10×$299 eligibility-ledger sales against DMS + Tax1099—otherwise remain Reject.
