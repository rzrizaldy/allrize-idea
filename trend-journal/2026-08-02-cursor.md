## Summary

**PhoneQuoteLog** is a narrow compliance web app for independent US funeral homes: keep a versioned General Price List (plus CPL/OBCPL), force staff through a telephone price-inquiry checklist, and produce an audit-ready log that the home answered itemized price questions from the *current* lists—without requiring caller identity. The duty is durable under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453), and the FTC’s first undercover phone sweep (2023 fieldwork; January 2024 warning letters to 39 homes after 250+ calls) proves enforcement attention on exactly this failure mode. The product is Stripe-monetizable and buildable by one strong developer. It is **Watch**, not Build: PrepRoom already sells FTC price-list modules at $179/month Pro, case-management and “workflow OS” vendors advertise GPL versioning, a paper/spreadsheet call log can occupy the same job, and no interviews yet show willingness to pay for a phone-only wedge separately from OSHA/FTC suites.

## Inference

- The phone-disclosure duty is a **durable process requirement**, not a fad: it is written into the Rule, reiterated in FTC staff guidance, measured in federal burden estimates, and stress-tested by an undercover sweep. Product urgency spikes after sweeps and warning letters more than from continuous search demand (Trends unavailable here).
- The sharpest solo-SaaS wedge is **not** another case-management OS. It is a **front-desk ritual**: open the call log → confirm the effective GPL version → answer from itemized lines (not package-only) → optional secure link to current GPL PDF → immutable timestamped record—usable by whoever answers the phone, including temporary staff.
- PrepRoom’s Pro tier anchors willingness-to-pay for *broader* funeral compliance (~$179/mo). A phone-only tool would need a much lower price ($29–$59/mo) or a one-time “sweep readiness” pack, and must prove PrepRoom/Passare do not already satisfy directors’ audit anxiety.
- AI should draft staff scripts and map spoken questions to GPL line items from structured lists—not invent prices. Hallucinated quotes would create the exact Funeral Rule exposure the product claims to prevent.
- Indonesia is not a credible near-term expansion: the FTC Funeral Rule is US-specific; no verified Indonesian analogue or distribution path was collected today.

## Assumptions

- A meaningful share of the ~15k independent homes still answer phones without a structured log tied to the *current* GPL version.
- Directors will pay a separate narrow tool rather than upgrade PrepRoom, rely on Passare/FrontRunner notes, or keep a binder/spreadsheet after reading FTC warning-letter coverage.
- Front-desk staff will actually use a checklist under call pressure; otherwise the audit trail is theater.
- Stripe card billing works for family-owned homes (no heavy PO procurement).
- Optional online GPL hosting remains a nice-to-have until any future FTC amendment; phone answering remains mandatory either way.
- State funeral-board rules do not forbid third-party SaaS storage of price lists (must confirm per state before multi-state marketing).

### Wedge

**Telephone inquiry ritual + GPL version pin + exportable evidence pack** for single-location independents who will not buy a full OSHA suite or case-management overhaul. Explicit non-goals for MVP: embalming ops, death-certificate filing, preneed trusts, website CMS, consumer marketplace.

### MVP

Multi-tenant web app a strong solo developer can ship:

1. Upload/edit GPL, CPL, OBCPL with effective dates and required-disclosure checklist against FTC guide headings.
2. One-click “start phone inquiry” form: timestamp, staff user, questions asked (structured tags), line items quoted, optional notes; **caller identity fields optional and never required to save**.
3. Pin the active price-list version hash/ID on every inquiry record.
4. Generate staff talk-track from itemized lines (AI-assisted, human-editable; never free-form price invention).
5. PDF/CSV export pack for a date range (sweep / counsel / insurer request).
6. Stripe Checkout subscription ($39/mo single location hypothesized) + optional $99 one-off “sweep readiness” setup.

### AI role

Workflow-specific: map natural-language questions (“how much for direct cremation?”) to the correct GPL rows and draft a compliant verbal answer; flag package-only answers; remind staff not to require caller ID. Reject generic chatbot wrapping of funeral content. Human remains responsible for the spoken answer.
