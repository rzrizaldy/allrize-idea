# Flat-Fee Dispute Evidence Workspace — 2026-07-30

**Contributor:** Cursor
**Market:** United States first; Indonesia relevance: secondary only for exporters/DTC brands selling into US card networks
**Verdict:** Watch

## Summary

Explore a Stripe-first, flat-fee **dispute evidence workspace** for US DTC and SaaS merchants who handle a modest dispute volume but lose winnable cases because shipping, support, and checkout evidence is scattered—and who dislike success-fee automation that auto-submits or takes ~25% of recovered amounts on high-AOV orders. The network rules and Stripe documentation make the workflow real and durable, but this is **not** promoted to Build today: Chargeflow, Justt, Stripe Smart Disputes / CE 3.0 tooling, and several open-source desks already occupy the space, vendor win-rate claims are not independently verified, and no primary interviews yet show willingness to pay a subscription instead of success-based recovery.

## Inference

- Card-network rules (especially Visa CE 3.0) and Stripe’s own evidence schema create a durable compliance workflow, not a short-lived AI fad. The durable product is **evidence assembly + deadline triage + human-controlled submit**, not a chatbot wrapper.
- A credible wedge vs success-fee automation is: merchants with **low monthly dispute count and/or high AOV** who want flat pricing, packet review before submit, and reason-code checklists mapped to Stripe fields—without granting a third party auto-submit authority.
- One strong developer can ship an MVP as a multi-tenant web app: Stripe Connect/OAuth + webhooks, reason-code checklist, file vault with size/page validators, PDF/packet builder, optional Shopify/carrier CSV import, and `disputes.update` with `submit:false` then explicit human submit. AI should only draft narratives and map uploads to fields from structured inputs.
- Incumbent economics (0 monthly fee + 25% of wins) are hard to beat for low-AOV / high-volume shops. Without interview proof that the flat-fee + control segment will pay, Build is premature.

## Assumptions

- A meaningful segment of US Stripe/Shopify merchants rejects success fees or auto-submit on disputes above a few hundred dollars.
- Merchants will connect Stripe (and optionally Shopify/helpdesk) rather than only paste CSVs.
- $39–$79/month or ~$19 per dispute is credible vs eating losses or paying 25% recovery fees.
- Operating a dispute-evidence product does not require card-network TPA registration if the merchant remains the submitting party (must be confirmed with counsel; AgentGuard’s public legal posture is not advice).
- Indonesia is only a secondary market if sellers clear US card transactions; domestic Indo payment rails are out of scope for v1.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

Dispute representment is a recurring cost of accepting cards. CE 3.0 is a network-rule evolution that raises the value of structured historical and device/IP evidence. Durability rests on payment-network process, not a search spike (Trends values unavailable here).

### Customer and JTBD

**Buyer:** founder/ops lead at a US DTC brand or B2B SaaS charging cards on Stripe (roughly mid–high AOV or subscription). **User:** whoever currently assembles dispute packets under deadline. **JTBD:** before the issuer deadline, assemble a reason-code-correct evidence packet from order, delivery, and support artifacts; review it; submit without losing the case to formatting or missing fields.

### Pain evidence

Stripe’s own docs show that even “best” predicted cases often lose (~40%+), that fraud is the majority category, and that reviewers punish irrelevant dumps. Vendor blogs consistently describe multi-system scavenger hunts. That supports process pain; it does **not** yet prove subscription WTP versus Chargeflow’s pay-on-win model. Interviews required.

### Alternatives

- Stripe Dashboard + Smart Disputes / Radar win scores / native CE 3.0 helpers.
- Success-fee automation: Chargeflow, Justt, and peers.
- Accounting/ops platforms that export order/tracking context (e.g., Webgility-style ops tools) without full representment.
- Manual Google Drive + spreadsheet checklists; local-first OSS desks.
- Doing nothing / accepting the dispute (still pays dispute fee; counts toward ratio).

Gap to test: **flat-fee, human-gated packet builder** for merchants who find success fees or black-box auto-submit unacceptable—not “can AI write a rebuttal.”

### Wedge

v1: Stripe-only workspace for **≤15 open disputes/month**, reason-code checklists, CE 3.0 eligibility surfacing when Stripe provides it, packet size linting to 4.5MB/page caps, and **review-then-submit**. Explicitly not prevention alerts, not Ethoca/Verifi, not “we guarantee wins.”

### MVP

- Stripe OAuth/webhook dispute inbox with deadline countdown and win-likelihood display when available.
- Per-reason evidence checklist mapped to Stripe evidence fields.
- Upload/combine PDFs/images with compression guidance; customer-communication concatenator.
- Optional CSV/manual attach for tracking numbers and helpdesk threads.
- Generate summary narrative + staged `submit:false` update; one-click submit after human confirm.
- Stripe Billing for flat monthly plan or per-dispute credits.

### AI role

AI drafts the short neutral narrative and suggests which uploaded files map to which Stripe fields / CE elements. It must not invent tracking, AVS, or prior-transaction matches. Durable value is schema mapping, deadlines, and auditability—not a generic “dispute GPT.”

### Pricing

**Hypothesis, not verified:** $49/month including 8 dispute packets, then $15/packet; or $99/month unlimited packets with fair-use cap. Concierge test: $79 for one live packet built with the merchant’s data in 48 hours.

### Distribution

Stripe App Marketplace listing, Indie Hackers / DTC Slack communities, and content SEO on “Stripe dispute evidence checklist” / CE 3.0 packet templates. Partner with Shopify fulfillment apps for delivery proof only after Stripe wedge works. Do not lead with Indonesia; optional later for Indo brands selling USD on Stripe.

### Risks

- **Market:** success-fee incumbents and Stripe-native tooling may be “good enough.”
- **Product:** without Shopify/carrier/helpdesk connectors, packet quality stays weak for INR (item not received) cases.
- **Technical:** webhook reliability, PII/security of dispute artifacts, file-size constraints.
- **Legal/network:** misrepresenting evidence, or operating in a way that triggers third-party agent obligations—require counsel.
- **Go-to-market:** dispute volume is lumpy; churn when a shop has a quiet quarter.

### Kill criteria

Stop after 15 qualified merchant interviews unless ≥6 describe ≥2 hours lost per dispute or a missed deadline in the last 90 days, and ≥3 prepay a $79 concierge packet or commit to $49/mo. Stop if targets consistently prefer Chargeflow-style success fees, or if Stripe-native CE 3.0 + Dashboard already closes their gap. Stop if counsel flags the submission model as requiring network registration the solo product cannot support.
