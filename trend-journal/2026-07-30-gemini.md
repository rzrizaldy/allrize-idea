## Summary

FlowGuard is a proposed mobile-first SaaS for independent certified backflow testers and small plumbing contractors. Its intended workflow is field entry of differential-pressure test results, generation of a water-purveyor report PDF, customer/property history, gauge-calibration reminders, and 60/30/14-day renewal outreach. Gemini Spark submitted this as a Build candidate in [the source document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XcHzIk_d2mO5TAv0PNMN2pq8-wkbadRc30UVpmrvGM0/edit?usp=drivesdk). This repository import records it as **Watch** because the source document does not provide traceable URLs for the claimed Google Trends result, customer-loss estimates, market count, fine ranges, or direct competitive gap.

## Inference

- A tester-facing system that retains the job history and triggers renewal outreach could be more focused than a general field-service product.
- The MVP is plausibly shippable by one strong developer: mobile forms, role-based customer/property records, PDF output, notifications, subscriptions, and an optional OCR prefill.
- The narrowest initial wedge is **one state or water-purveyor reporting format**, not a nationwide “compliant PDF” claim. Requirements vary across local jurisdictions.

## Assumptions

- The Google Trends claim of “+35% YoY search volume” in the source document is not verified: no query URL, date range, geography, or export was supplied.
- The claims of “over 100,000” testers, 10–15 minutes saved per test, 20%–30% recurring-customer loss, $80–$250 test prices, and $250–$1,000/day fines are not verified in the supplied material.
- The Safe Drinking Water Act and model plumbing codes do **not**, by themselves, establish a uniform nationwide annual-testing requirement for all listed customers. Local/state rules and water-purveyor requirements must be checked for the selected launch market.
- It is unverified whether target purveyors accept third-party PDF reports, which formats they require, and whether testers will pay $49–$99/month instead of using existing tools.
- Indonesia/SEA expansion has not been validated and should not shape the US MVP.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

Water-safety and compliance workflows may be recurring, but no durable demand signal has yet been established. Re-run Google Trends with an explicit US query and retain its URL, timeframe, and exported/observed result.

### Customer and JTBD

**Proposed buyer/user:** a solo certified backflow tester, master plumber, or 1–5 technician plumbing shop.  
**Proposed JTBD:** record a field test once, create the locally accepted report, and bring the same customer back before the next due date.

### Pain evidence

The source identifies manual forms, retyping, rejected reports, and missed renewal reminders as possible pain. These remain hypotheses until interviews, portal requirements, or primary utility/tester evidence is collected.

### Alternatives

- Paper forms, spreadsheets, calendar reminders, and generic field-service software.
- Water-purveyor portals and the named utility-side platforms.
- Existing specialised backflow products, which must be mapped state by state before claiming a product gap.

### Wedge

A state-specific, tester-side test form and customer re-test queue, starting with a local report format that is explicitly confirmed as accepted by the relevant purveyor. It must not claim legal or compliance approval.

### MVP

- Mobile test-entry form for one selected assembly/reporting workflow.
- Customer, property, assembly, serial number, and test-history record.
- Human-reviewed PDF report export with signature capture.
- Email/SMS re-test reminders and gauge-calibration dates.
- Stripe checkout.
- Optional OCR to prefill a notice or form, always reviewable by the tester.

### AI role

OCR may extract candidate fields from a customer notice. It must not decide technical compliance, interpret a test result, or replace tester/purveyor review.

### Pricing

**Hypothesis:** $49/month starter, $99/month pro, or $3/report for seasonal volume. Validate against a paid concierge pilot before building metered billing.

### Distribution

State-certified tester directories and local plumbing-supply counters are candidate channels. Their accessibility, list quality, and conversion economics are unverified.

### Risks

- Local rules and report formats may fragment the product.
- Existing platforms or portals may already meet the job.
- The target audience may resist new software.
- SMS consent, document retention, and security require careful design.
- Incorrect report generation could cause operational harm; keep human review and no compliance guarantee.

### Kill criteria

Do not promote to Build unless:
1. 15 qualified testers across a deliberately chosen launch state are interviewed;
2. at least 6 confirm a current recurring admin/renewal problem and a gap in their existing tool;
3. at least 3 agree to a paid concierge pilot at or above $39/month equivalent;
4. two or more target purveyors/testers confirm the first PDF/report workflow is accepted; and
5. competitor review shows a credible, specific wedge.
