# ExitGI Desk — Medicare Advantage plan-exit book triage + Medigap GI-window packet for independent US agents — 2026-08-31

**Contributor:** Cursor
**File:** `trend-journal/2026-08-31-cursor.md`
**Market:** United States first; Indonesia relevance: not yet established (Medicare Advantage / Medigap is a US federal program with no credible Indonesian secondary market)
**Motion:** B2B. Independent Medicare insurance agents and small agencies pay; licensed agents use it to triage books, sequence outreach, and document enrollment-period deadlines. Beneficiaries are end users of advice but not the paying customer.
**Verdict:** Watch

## Summary

Humana confirmed on its July 29, 2026 earnings call that 2027 Medicare Advantage plan exits will affect about **600,000** members, with management expecting to recapture just over **40%** into other Humana plans. Non-renewal / ANOC letters land in late September–early October; AEP runs October 15–December 7, 2026; plan-nonrenewal SEP and a **63-day Medigap guaranteed-issue** window open distinct clocks that agents routinely mishandle relative to AEP. Google Trends Trending Now listed **`humana medicare advantage cuts 2027`** at **10K+** US searches on 2026-08-31. The durable job is real—carrier exits are industry-wide, not a one-day celebrity spike—but the exact workflow (carrier/book audit → wait for ANOC → compliant outreach → Medigap GI calendar) is already sold by Medicare-specific CRMs at SMB prices ($99–$199/mo) and by FMOs as free CRM + playbooks. A solo Stripe web app that only matches exits and prints GI deadlines does not yet clear a Build bar until prepaid demand beats Excel + free FMO CRM + MedicareCopilot-class tools, and until the founder can carry HIPAA for book-of-business PHI.

## Inference

- Search and trade-press attention around Humana exits will spike again when ANOC letters hit mailboxes; the underlying carrier-margin / Star / medical-cost pressure that forces plan exits looks multi-year, so **demand for exit-season ops is more durable than the named Humana query**.
- The narrowest unmet *feeling* is not “need another CRM.” It is “tell me which of my Humana (and other exit) clients actually lost a plan, compute AEP/SEP/GI clocks, and force a Medigap GI conversation before I re-shop MA”—a seasonal packet, not a 12-month suite.
- Incumbents already claim AEP renewal tracking, digital SOA/PTC, and book import. FMOs already publish free playbooks and free CRMs for the pre-ANOC audit. That collapses willingness to pay for a thin overlay unless agents explicitly refuse CRM migration and still miss GI windows in measurable volume.
- HIPAA and CMS marketing rules raise the cost of a solo product that stores PHI or automates beneficiary contact. A local-first / export-only CSV tool reduces liability but also reduces lock-in and perceived value versus MedicareCopilot.
- Indonesia is not a secondary market: there is no analogous MA/Medigap GI workflow for Indonesian buyers.

## Assumptions

- A meaningful slice of independent agents still keep books in spreadsheets or generic CRMs that cannot filter by carrier/plan/county in minutes.
- When CMS Landscape / crosswalk / carrier notices become machine-readable for 2027 exits, matching them to agent CSVs will be accurate enough to trust without counsel review of every row.
- Agents will pay a seasonal **$79–$149** one-time fee (or ~$29/mo for Sep–Feb) rather than adopt a $99/mo full CRM or use an FMO’s free tool—**unverified**.
- Automated or templated outreach can stay inside CMS marketing rules with agent review gates; if not, the product shrinks to a deadline calculator with weak differentiation.
- Humana’s county/plan exit list will become public on a schedule that leaves a usable software window before AEP closes.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

**Durable industry process, episodic named query.** Carrier MA exits and ANOC-driven reshuffles have repeated (Humana cited its 2025 trim; TMS cites broader 2027 displacement estimates). The Google Trends `humana medicare advantage cuts 2027` item is a news-attention spike that will likely reappear around letter mail and AEP, not a permanent search category. Software demand, if any, is seasonal (Aug prep → Feb SEP) rather than evergreen SaaS MRR—unless packaged as a recurring “exit season” subscription across carriers.

### Customer and JTBD

**Motion:** B2B. **Buyer:** independent Medicare agent or small agency owner (often FMO-aligned). **User:** same agent / downline producers. **JTBD:** “Before ANOC chaos, know which clients sit on exit-risk carriers; once letters land, prioritize outreach, explain AEP vs SEP vs Medigap GI without missing the 63-day underwriting bypass, and keep an audit trail of what I told whom.”

### Pain evidence

- ~600k Humana members alone; agents are told ~60% of that cohort may be “in play” after ~40% Humana recapture.
- Playbooks warn that panic messaging before counties are public destroys trust, while waiting without templates burns the Oct–Dec 7 window.
- Medigap GI is described as a rare underwriting bypass that beneficiaries and busy agents miss when they default to another $0-premium MA plan.
- Pain is mostly **seller/FMO-voiced** (playbooks, CRM marketing). First-person agent complaints about paying for a *standalone* exit matcher were not collected in this run.

### Wedge

**ExitGI Desk:** CSV book import → tag Humana/other-exit carriers → (post-ANOC) match published exit plans/counties → per-client deadline card (AEP end, SEP end, Medigap GI end) → checklist that blocks “new MA quote” until Medigap GI options are marked reviewed → exportable evidence ZIP (timestamps, template versions, agent attestation). Explicitly **not** a quoting engine, dialer, or AI benefits chatbot.

### MVP

- Stripe Checkout: **$99** one-time “2027 Exit Season” seat (Sep 2026–Feb 2027) or **$29/mo** for up to 6 months.
- CSV import (name, phone, zip/county, carrier, contract/plan IDs if known).
- Manual + file-based exit list upload until CMS/carrier feeds stabilize.
- Deadline engine for AEP/SEP/GI with state Medigap GI caveat flags (NY/CT/MA/ME called out in press).
- Template library placeholders (email/phone/postcard) marked “compliance review required—not legal advice.”
- PDF/ZIP packet export; optional local-only mode to reduce PHI hosting.
- No auto-SMS to beneficiaries in v1 (CMS marketing risk).

### AI role

Optional later: OCR/parse ANOC PDFs clients photograph; draft plain-language explanations of the three clocks. **Not** the core product. Reject generic “AI Medicare advisor” wrappers—MedicareCopilot already markets AI plan scoring and chat.

### Pricing

Hypothesis: agents who refuse a $99/mo CRM might still buy a **$79–$149** seasonal packet if it saves one retained book or one Medigap GI placement. Competing free FMO tools make this hypothesis fragile—validate with prepaid deposits before build.

### Distribution

- August–September SEO/content around “Humana nonrenewal agent checklist” and “Medigap GI after MA exit.”
- FMO/IMO partnership as a white-label prep pack (distribution strong; margin and brand weak).
- Facebook/LinkedIn agent groups and AEP webinars—high spam competition.
- Do not buy beneficiary leads; buyer is the agent.

### Risks

- **Market:** Full CRMs and free FMO tools close the job.
- **Product:** Exit lists arrive as PDFs/letters, not clean APIs; match quality fails.
- **Legal:** HIPAA/PHI hosting; CMS marketing and SOA rules; product must not give legal/coverage advice.
- **GTM:** Seasonal; miss Aug–Oct and the year is gone.
- **Technical:** Solo founder carrying BAAs, audit logs, and encryption is heavier than a Stripe CSV toy.

### Kill criteria

Stop if any of the following:

1. Fewer than **10** prepaid **$79+** deposits from licensed agents in 14 days of a landing-page test after a sample exit-county file is demonstrable.
2. Agents report they already complete the same packet in FMO CRM / MedicareCopilot in under 15 minutes.
3. Counsel review says storing book PHI or generating outreach templates creates unacceptable CMS/HIPAA exposure for a solo operator.
4. 2027 Landscape / carrier notices never yield a matchable machine-readable exit set before Oct 15.

## Verdict rationale

**Watch, not Build.** Trend and regulatory clocks are real; JTBD is clear; MVP is solo-buildable. But named SMB incumbents already sell Medicare CRM + AEP compliance at transparent prices, FMOs give away the book-audit workflow, and PHI/CMS risk is high. Revisit only if prepaid ExitGI demand appears after ANOC county lists publish and agents still refuse full CRM adoption.

**No `idea-grill/` promotion** (Build-only rule).
