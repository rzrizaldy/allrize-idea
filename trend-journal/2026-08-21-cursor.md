## Summary

**FiveClean** would be a Stripe-monetizable evidence workspace for getting a firm or product **off** an FDA Import Alert red list (Detention Without Physical Examination / DWPE). It would turn the official petition checklist—root-cause investigation, corrective and preventive actions, verification artifacts, and typically **five consecutive non-violative commercial entries** with entry numbers, dates, ports, and private-lab results—into a tracked case file and exportable exhibit pack for email to FDA’s Division of Import Operations (commonly `ImportAlerts2@fda.hhs.gov`, subject to the alert’s Guidance section).

Today’s US Google Trends spike is consumer search around the Publix GreenWise frozen-berry Class I upgrade. The durable solo-SaaS job is not another recall alert feed or full FSVP suite. It is the high-stakes, multi-month **red-list exit** workflow that follows when FDA adds a foreign packer (here, a Chilean blueberry firm) to Import Alert **99-35**. The wedge is buildable for one strong developer, but prepaid willingness to pay versus food-safety counsel and consultants is unverified, buyers are episodic, and the product must not claim FDA approval. **Watch**, not Build; not promoted to `idea-grill/`.

### DWPE removal workflow (official + practitioner)

- FDA’s Industry FAQs state firms may petition for removal from DWPE/import alert (commonly via `importalerts2@fda.hhs.gov`), that there is **no single mandatory evidence list**, and that FDA considers the **totality of evidence**; some alerts (e.g. **99-41**) route petitions to a case compliance officer. FAQs point to the [Removal from DWPE Under Import Alert](https://www.fda.gov/industry/import-alerts/removal-dwpe-under-import-alert) page. [Industry FAQs](https://www.fda.gov/industry/import-alerts/industry-faqs-import-alerts)
- Search-indexed content of that Removal page (collected **2026-08-21**; direct HTML fetch returned 404 from this environment) describes Red List petition themes: investigation, corrective actions, preventive measures, and evidence such as **five clean shipments** or a third-party audit; petitions commonly email `ImportAlerts2@fda.hhs.gov` unless Guidance says otherwise.
- Example alert Guidance (seafood Import Alert **16-22**) explicitly recommends investigation results, corrective-action documentation, and **a minimum of five consecutive import entries released by FDA based on non-violative private laboratory analyses**, with petitions to DIO / `Importalerts2@fda.hhs.gov`, and points to RPM Chapter 9-8. [Import Alert 16-22](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/CMS_IA/importalert_39.html)
- The Acheson Group describes removal as costly and complex: hold-and-test each shipment, private-lab costs borne by the company, multi-week clearance per entry, and a petition that usually needs root cause, CAPA/verification, and **five consecutive compliant shipments**; incomplete petitions are often denied. [Acheson Group](https://achesonfoodsafetyconsulting.com/import-alert-removal-a-costly-and-complex-process/)

## Inference

- The Trends spike is consumer Class I attention. The solo-SaaS angle is the **importer/supplier exit** job created when FDA couples outbreak investigation with **Import Alert 99-35** DWPE.
- Monitoring at $19/mo and FSVP OS products leave a plausible gap: a **case-scoped petition vault** that tracks the five-entry sequence (entry #, port, lab COA, FDA release), CAPA documents, and a counsel-reviewed cover-letter/exhibit ZIP.
- AI’s useful role is extraction and checklist mapping (OCR lab PDFs → shipment rows; draft exhibit index), not automated legal advice or “FDA will approve” claims.
- Buyer is episodic and high-stakes; counsel may insist on owning the narrative. That favors **Watch** until interviews show prepaid demand for a self-serve desk beneath consultant retainers.
- Indonesia is only a secondary angle for exporters facing US DWPE; domestic Indonesian food-safety software is a different product.

## Assumptions

- Enough small/mid produce and specialty-food importers face DWPE each year to support a niche SaaS without becoming a custom consulting shop.
- Buyers will pay for software **in addition to** counsel/labs rather than only buying services.
- Alert-specific Guidance differences (five shipments vs audit-only vs 99-41 officer routing) can be templated without becoming unbounded legal research.
- Stripe self-serve checkout is acceptable for this buyer (vs PO-only procurement).
- The product can operate as a document vault + checklist without storing perishable product or making safety determinations.

## Consulting assessment

### Trend durability

Class I consumer spikes are episodic. DWPE and Import Alert red lists are a durable FDA border tool; removal petitions recur whenever firms are listed. Durability rests on FDA process, not the Publix search spike.

### Customer and JTBD

**Motion:** B2B (foreign manufacturer/packer and/or US importer pays; regulatory/QA and counsel use). **JTBD:** assemble a complete, source-linked DWPE removal petition so FDA has confidence future entries will comply—without restarting the five-shipment clock because an exhibit was missing.

### Pain evidence

Official FAQs + alert Guidance + practitioner write-ups show multi-week detentions, private-lab cost per entry, and petition denials for missing CAPA or five-entry proof. That is strong process pain; it is not yet prepaid SaaS WTP.

### Alternatives

Spreadsheets + counsel email; Import Alert Radar / Suppliwatch monitoring; FSVP platforms; full-service consultancies. No SMB self-serve **petition evidence desk** with public Stripe pricing was found in this review—absence is not proof of an empty category.

### Wedge

Start with **produce / Import Alert 99-35-style insanitary-conditions** exit packs: shipment timeline + lab COA vault + CAPA checklist + exhibit ZIP for DIO email—not FSVP program software and not consumer recall alerts.

### MVP

Multi-tenant web app: create case (alert #, firm, products); checklist from FDA Red List themes; upload RCA/CAPA/audit/label PDFs; log five entries (entry number, date, port, lab result, release status); generate cover-letter stub + numbered exhibit index + ZIP; Stripe Checkout for per-case fee. Human review required before send; no automated FDA filing API.

### AI role

Parse lab PDFs and detention notices into structured fields; draft exhibit indices and plain-language gap lists from the checklist. Deterministic validation of required fields; no compliance certification.

### Pricing

Hypothesis only: **$499–$1,499 per removal case** (or $99/mo while a case is open) versus multi-month detention costs and consultant petition retainers. Validate against Acheson-class service pricing in interviews.

### Distribution

Customs brokers, private labs that already run DWPE hold-and-test work, produce-importer associations, and content SEO around “import alert removal petition checklist” / “five consecutive shipments FDA.”

### Risks

Legal/product: buyers treat outputs as FDA-ready; alert Guidance variance; counsel channel conflict; tiny episodic TAM; confusion with FSVP or recall products already scouted (RecallBinder / RecallDrill / Import Alert Radar).

### Kill criteria

- Interviews show buyers will only pay counsel and refuse a software fee.
- A named SMB tool ships the exact petition-pack wedge at similar price.
- FDA changes removal practice so five-entry documentation is rarely used for target alerts.
- Case volume cannot support acquisition cost within 90 days of a landing-page test.
