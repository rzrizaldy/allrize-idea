## Target and MVP

**Target:** 1–10 person marketing, RevOps, data, and AI automation agencies.

**MVP:** A client workspace with CSV KPI upload, goal-to-evidence mapping, a report generator, PDF or share-link delivery, and Stripe billing.

## Pricing

- $39/month solo
- $99/month agency
- $49 one-off proof pack

## Verified evidence

- The product requirements above are the requested initial product definition. Independent customer-demand evidence has not yet been collected in this journal.

## Inference

- Small agencies and AI consultants need a repeatable way to connect reported outcomes to source evidence, which can make retention and renewal conversations easier.
- A narrow report-generation workflow can be shipped as a focused web app by one strong developer and offers a direct Stripe monetization path.

## Assumptions

- Target agencies will upload CSV exports and pay for evidence-linked monthly reporting.
- The proposed price points match willingness to pay.
- A client-ready PDF or share link is a sufficiently compelling wedge against spreadsheets, slides, and generic reporting tools.

## 7-day validation plan

1. Day 1: Create a one-page landing page and three report mockups for marketing, RevOps, and AI automation agencies.
2. Day 2: Build a list of 30 target agencies and identify the owner or client-success lead for each.
3. Days 3–4: Conduct 10 structured interviews focused on current reporting workflow, time spent, evidence gaps, and renewal pressure.
4. Day 5: Offer five interviewees a concierge proof pack using their anonymized or sample CSV data for $49.
5. Day 6: Deliver the packs, measure revision requests, and ask for a monthly pilot at $39 or $99.
6. Day 7: Review conversion, workflow fit, and objections; document evidence in a dated trend-journal entry before expanding the build.

## Kill criteria

- Fewer than 3 of 10 qualified interviews identify an evidence-to-impact reporting pain worth solving.
- Fewer than 2 of 5 concierge offers accept a paid $49 proof pack or a clear commitment to a paid pilot.
- Target users consistently prefer their existing spreadsheet, slide, or reporting tool without naming a material evidence gap.
- Manual delivery requires more than 90 minutes per client per month without a credible path to reduce it in the MVP.
